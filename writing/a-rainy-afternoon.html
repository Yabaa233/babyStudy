A Rainy Afternoon

Author: Partner B · 2026-10-25 · 待互改

It started raining just after lunch. The sky turned gray, and the streets became quiet. I made a cup of tea and sat by the window with a novel.

Outside, people walked quickly with umbrellas. A small dog shook water from its fur near the shop door. I felt calm. Sometimes a rainy afternoon is the best time to slow down and practice English by reading aloud.
