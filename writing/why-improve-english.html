Why I Want to Improve My English

Author: Partner A · 2026-10-27 · 待互改

I want to improve my English for three reasons. First, I hope to watch films and read books without subtitles. Second, English helps me talk with people from other countries. Third, studying with a partner makes the journey less lonely and more fun.

This site is our small classroom. We add words, set weekly goals, and correct each other's writing. Step by step, I believe we will both become more confident.
