My Study Routine

Author: Partner A · 2026-10-22 · 已互改

Every morning I spend twenty minutes on vocabulary. I write each new word in a notebook and make one sentence. In the evening I listen to a short podcast and repeat useful phrases out loud.

On weekends my study buddy and I meet online. We choose one speaking topic and talk for fifteen minutes. After that we leave short feedback on each other's writing. This routine is simple, but it helps me stay consistent.
